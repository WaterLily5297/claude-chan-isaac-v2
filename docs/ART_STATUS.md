# Art inventory and work queue

Art inventory audited from source `8fd6a8c` on 2026-10-26, updated through the [ninth icon batch](ITEM_ART_BATCH_09.md) on 2026-10-27. The [92-icon registry](item-art-manifest.json) now records all 92 icons as designed; no placeholder icon remains. The nine batches add offline icon review, not a new character or engine acceptance pass. The owner has not tested the installed `8fd6a8c`. Historical observations remain in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md) and [POSE_REVIEW.md](POSE_REVIEW.md).

## Scope and evidence

The runtime and earlier review inventory contains **216 tracked art files: 191 PNGs and 25 ANM2 files**. The tables below enumerate those paths once: 22 gameplay sheets, 22 stage/VS portraits, 22 dossier portraits, 22 dossier wrappers, 11 shared name strips, six menu files, 80 collectible icons, 12 trinket icons, one repository thumbnail and eighteen item-review PNGs. The [approved tainted design registry](approved-tainted-designs.json) separately inventories nine high-resolution source PNGs and their comparison JPEG, bringing that checkpoint to 226 files. The [GLM pose repair](GLM_PROFILE_POSES_2026-10-31.md) adds two generated source PNGs and one review PNG: **229 art files total (203 PNGs, 25 ANM2, one JPEG)**. The [GPT/Gemini profile repair](GPT_GEMINI_PROFILE_POSES_2026-10-31.md) adds two source PNGs and two review PNGs: **233 art files total (207 PNGs, 25 ANM2, one JPEG)**. The [GLM carry-fit follow-up](GLM_CARRY_CLEARANCE_2026-10-31.md) adds one review PNG: **234 art files total (208 PNGs, 25 ANM2, one JPEG)**. The [Grok/Qwen repair](GROK_QWEN_CARRY_PROFILES_2026-10-31.md) adds four generated sources, one original geometry guide and two reviews: **241 art files total (215 PNGs, 25 ANM2, one JPEG)**. The [Llama repair](LLAMA_CARRY_PROFILES_2026-10-31.md) adds two generated sources and one review: **244 art files total (218 PNGs, 25 ANM2, one JPEG)**. These twenty-eight additional review/source files are not loaded by the game. No font file is bundled. The five pills, Relay Slime and native effect/HUD presentation are listed separately because they do not add custom image files.

On 2026-10-31 the owner approved all nine distinct tainted full-body designs. [The approval and production handoff](TAINTED_DESIGN_APPROVAL_2026-10-31.md) records the exact sources and remaining dossier/menu/gameplay adaptation. The runtime C-D assets below still use the old darkening output; approval does not mean those replacements have shipped. The existing generator/importer overlaps open v2 PR #2, so production integration must wait for that dependency without duplicating its work.

Counts, PNG headers, XML registrations and generator/manifests were inspected offline. Reported anatomy issues come from the earlier O5 review; the six item batches render and review their sixty icons and original reference shapes. Geometry, exact regenerated bytes and provenance records alone do not establish aesthetic quality, licence clearance beyond the recorded terms, or in-game compatibility.
