All **97 registered rows remain open**: 80 collectibles (36 active, 44 passive), 12 trinkets and five pills. Three unregistered systems are listed separately. The 50-item expansion is included exactly once; meme extras already registered as items are not counted again.

The owner has not tested installed 8fd6a8c (gameplay ef7d2dd). That installation is preserved. No game, runtime, settings or saves were accessed for this audit. Earlier engine evidence retains its own build and scope in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

The 2026-10-27 [ordinary primary-tear matrix](PRIMARY_TEAR_COMPOSITION.md) adds six-item combination evidence without closing any row. Its real item callbacks cover the four item tints; `native_tear_color` covers character identity tints and is no longer cited as direct item-composition evidence. The 2026-10-27 [charm/shield correction](CHARM_DAMAGE_ORDER.md) resolves one F9 source-order defect, while preserving beneficial-proc flag behavior and all broader acceptance limits.

## Reading the ledger

Item names link to the principal implementation; shared behavior also lives in [main.lua](../main.lua), [backlog common](../scripts/items/backlog/common.lua), [MemeState](../scripts/items/meme_state.lua), [MealBreak](../scripts/meal_break.lua) and [Risk](../scripts/risk.lua). The [generated reference](ITEMS.md) preserves exact pickup/EID text; [expansion design](EXPANSION_DESIGN.md) and [batch 2 rules](MEME_BATCH_2.md) are intended targets. A recorded target/code discrepancy is not permission to invent a fix or revive an abandoned rule.

Registration shorthand: A4 means active with four room charges, A150t means timed maxcharges 150, P means passive, T means trinket, Pill means pill effect. Q is XML quality. Pool names are literal XML entries, not observed spawn frequencies. All 80 pool rows have Weight 1, DecreaseBy 1 and RemoveOn 0.1: treasure 56, shop 11, devil 7, angel 6. They cover 79 unique collectibles; Residential IP occurs twice. Lunch Break has no collectible-pool row and is assigned as DeepSeek's starting pocket item. No pool change is proposed. Trinkets and pills have registrations, not collectible-pool rows.

Only Ban Hammer, Summary Stapler, Ignore All Previous Instructions and I Panicked declare the offensive tag. Other native interaction tags are not explicitly authored. Absence is not proof of incompatibility; verify native behavior before changing tags. At the initial mechanism audit all 92 icons were placeholders. The later [first](ITEM_ART_BATCH_01.md), [second](ITEM_ART_BATCH_02.md), [third](ITEM_ART_BATCH_03.md), [fourth](ITEM_ART_BATCH_04.md), [fifth](ITEM_ART_BATCH_05.md) and [sixth](ITEM_ART_BATCH_06.md) art batches replace sixty with designed icons; 32 remain placeholders, and pills still use native presentation. This art change does not close any mechanism/native row. [ART_STATUS.md](ART_STATUS.md) and [ART_SPEC.md](ART_SPEC.md) track actual art completion separately.

Each fixture link names existing offline evidence for a relevant branch, not full coverage of the row. In particular, smoke_test.lua often prints observed values and lacks a dedicated assertion for a claimed interaction. Registration/EID/reference tests and the full 69-Lua-suite gate passed at the audit baseline, together with 11 Python scanner cases. No 97-case native matrix was run.
